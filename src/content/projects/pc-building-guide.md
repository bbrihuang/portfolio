## Overview

A step-by-step guide that takes someone from a list of spec sheets to a working computer. I wrote it to bridge the gap between what component specs say and what you actually do with your hands.

## What it covers

- Picking parts that work together without one bottlenecking another
- Assembly steps and wiring safety
- BIOS setup: XMP/EXPO memory profiles, fan curves, and boot order
- Troubleshooting a PC that won't boot or won't show a display
- Positive vs. negative pressure airflow in a case
