## Overview

I'm a Nozzle Design Engineer on the Boston University Rocket Propulsion Group. I work on nozzle geometry, heat handling, and structural integrity for the team's prototype rocket engines.

## What I work on

- Modeling nozzle geometry in CAD and iterating on it through team design reviews
- Evaluating materials for the extreme temperatures and pressures inside the nozzle
- Coordinating with the electrical and feed-system teams on how the nozzle assembly integrates with the rest of the engine
- Preparing components for fabrication and static-fire testing
