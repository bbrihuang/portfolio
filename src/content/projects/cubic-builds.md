## Overview

I started Cubic Builds to build custom computers for people who need more than an off-the-shelf machine: gamers, video editors, and people running workstation software.

## What I do

- Talk with each client about what they'll use the machine for and their budget
- Choose compatible components and plan airflow and cooling around the workload
- Assemble the system, including custom cable routing and liquid cooling loops
- Tune BIOS settings, memory timings, and overclocks, then stress test before delivery
- Handle warranty issues and ongoing hardware support afterward

## Results

Generated $1,000+ in revenue selling builds to clients locally and nationwide.
